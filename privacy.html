Alpine Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Alpine Quiz is designed to keep your quiz experience primarily on your device. This page explains what data the app stores and how it is used.

1. Overview

Alpine Quiz does not require account creation and does not sell your personal information. The app is built to work with local progress storage for gameplay features such as XP, streaks, achievements, round history, review rounds, and preferences.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store gameplay and preference data locally using standard iOS app storage, including:

  • XP, ELO, achievements, streaks, and lifetime stats
  • Recent rounds, missed-question review history, and selected quiz settings
  • Preferences such as difficulty, category, region, and haptic feedback settings

This information is used only to provide app functionality and personalize your local gameplay experience.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

Alpine Quiz does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information through in-app account systems. The app does not share your saved progress with third parties and does not upload your local quiz history to external servers as part of normal app use.

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

Alpine Quiz does not include its own account-based tracking or mandatory analytics by default. If platform providers such as Apple collect limited diagnostic information through system-level settings, that processing is controlled by those providers and your device settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your progress remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete all locally stored app progress and preferences at any time by opening the app and going to Settings > Reset All Progress.

6. Children's Privacy

Alpine Quiz is a general-audience trivia game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration or direct in-app submission forms.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app changes, legal updates, or operational improvements. The latest version should be considered the current policy.
