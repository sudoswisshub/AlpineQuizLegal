Need help with gameplay, saved progress, or app behavior? This page covers the most common issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my progress?

Open the app, tap the gear icon, and choose Reset All Progress. This removes saved local progress, achievements, settings, and recent round history.

Why am I not feeling haptic feedback?

Check that your device supports haptics, confirm system haptics are enabled on iPhone, and make sure the in-app Haptic Feedback setting is turned on.

How do I unlock new canton regions?

Region unlocks are tied to progression. Earn XP, improve your tier, and additional canton regions will become available over time.

How does the review mode work?

Questions you miss or skip can reappear in the app’s review practice flow. This helps you replay weaker topics and improve category mastery.

Troubleshooting

If the app seems stuck or data looks out of sync, try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue continues, include the following in your support email:

Device model, iOS version, what you tapped before the issue happened, and whether the issue affects saved progress or only the current round.
